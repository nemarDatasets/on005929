## Contributed notes (version 1.0.2)

- fNIRS recordings of 7 participants performing instructed head and body movements (motion-artifact task) with collodion-fixed prism-based optical fibres (Yücel et al. 2014, NeuroImage 85:192-201, doi:10.1016/j.neuroimage.2013.06.054).
- Time unit: the SNIRF files of all 7 recordings declare the time unit "unknown". Read as seconds, their time axes give a sampling rate of 50 Hz, equal to SamplingFrequency in every nirs.json; nothing points to milliseconds.
